Scriptoscope Reference Native Theme — concept prompt

Create a 4:3 concept illustration of a classic Mac OS Kaleidoscope theme.
A kingfisher with a teal back, orange breast, white cheek and strong beak
perches on a branch forming the upper rim of a frosted-glass window.
A calm water band with a central ripple anchors the lower edge.

Use pale ice-white glass, blue-grey outlines, restrained lavender/blue bevels,
teal accents and an olive branch. Keep the bird distinctive and the rest quiet.
Use crisp pixel edges, binary transparency, a limited palette and upper-left
lighting. Ornaments must fit inside the rectangular frame's outer bounds.

Show an active document window titled "Document Window" and a quieter inactive
window behind it on a plain grey background. Include legible close, zoom and
collapse widgets, scroll bars, Cancel and default OK buttons, checkboxes and
radio buttons. Preserve enough clear title space at narrow widths.

The saved concept.jpg is the visual target. Exact dimensions, colours and
resource geometry belong to the implementation, not this prompt. See brief.md
for the translation rules and details that must survive implementation.
