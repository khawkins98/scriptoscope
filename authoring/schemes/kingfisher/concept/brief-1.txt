Theme name HALCYON, title text "Document Window". Big idea: a kingfisher's riverbank on a frosted modern desktop. Materials: frosted ice-white glass chrome with a cool pale blue-grey tint, a thin kingfisher-teal accent, and a calm water band. Signature ornaments: a chunky pixel-art kingfisher bird (teal back, orange breast, long beak) perched on the top-left corner of the window ABOVE the title bar on a short reed or branch that also runs along the top as the bar's upper edge; the bottom edge is a calm water band with a stylised ripple and one reflection; the title sits on a frosted pane with rounded ends. Widgets are small frosted discs with teal glyphs. Palette: ice white #ffffff, pale glass #ccddee / #99aacc, slate outline #336699-ish dark, kingfisher teal #0099cc and #006699, breast orange #ff9933, reed olive #669933. Keep it calm and clean but characterful, like a modern desktop that a bird landed on. Style: a concept illustration of a classic Mac OS window theme mockup, 1990s Kaleidoscope style, crisp pixel-art look, flat plain background (solid mid-grey #8a94a8), no drop shadows, no photo textures. The illustration must show: one ACTIVE document window in front with a title bar reading the given title, three small title-bar widgets (close at left, zoom and collapse at right, each about 13 pixels, crisp and legible), a vertical and a horizontal scroll bar with arrows and a thumb, and inside the window two push buttons (Cancel, OK with a default ring), three checkboxes (one checked) and two radio buttons; plus one INACTIVE window partly behind it in a quieter palette. Window chrome must stay inside a rectangular footprint: ornaments may rise above the title bar as a taller top band, and may sit at the corners or repeat along edges, but nothing hangs outside the rectangle. Use a limited palette (under 40 colours), hard pixel edges, one light direction from the upper left, 1-pixel highlight rows and dark outlines rather than soft gradients.
