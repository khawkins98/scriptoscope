Theme name CASTLE, title text "Document Window". Big idea: the window IS a stone keep, and the hanging heraldic banner IS the title bar (there is no separate grey title bar: the banner with the text "Document Window" hangs from the crenellated wall and the wall row is the title bar; the three widgets sit directly on the wall stones at the far left (a heraldic shield = close) and far right (a square window opening = zoom, a raised drawbridge chevron = collapse)). Materials: chunky bevelled grey ashlar stone, 14 pixel blocks on 6 pixel courses, only about a third of the blocks with a lit top edge, dark mortar, a little moss at the base; one light from the upper left so the right tower is in shade. Signature ornaments: two round corner towers with conical red-tiled roofs and small pennants rising above the wall; merlons along the top between the towers; the banner is deep red cloth with gold fringe hung on a wooden pole with finials; arrow slits down the side walls; the bottom edge is a plinth over a narrow moat, with a portcullis gate in the lower-right corner as the resize box. Push buttons are oak planks with iron bands, the default button has a brass ring; the scroll thumb is a bound oak barrel, scroll arrows are stone plates with carved arrowheads; checkboxes are small iron-bound plates with a red X, radios are round shield bosses. Body is cream parchment. Style: a concept illustration of a classic Mac OS window theme mockup, 1990s Kaleidoscope style, crisp pixel-art look, flat plain background (solid mid-grey #8a94a8), no drop shadows, no photo textures.
