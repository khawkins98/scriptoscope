Theme name BRICKWORKS, title text "Document Window". Big idea: a window built from generic toy construction bricks on a toy construction site (NO brand names, logos, minifigures or printed studs). Materials: flat glossy plastic in primary colours, a single 1 pixel gloss row along each brick top, hard warm-dark outlines, cylindrical studs (each stud: a lit top ring, flat side, 1 pixel shade). Signature ornaments: a big yellow 2x2 corner block top-left whose studs rise above the title bar; a yellow toy crane arm (lattice boom with a hook on a string) standing on the top-right corner block and rising above the bar, holding a dangling single red brick; the title bar is a run of red 2x4 bricks with studs; the title plate is a white tile brick with a thin dark outline; the side walls are staggered red and blue brick courses with visible seams; the bottom is a green studded baseplate with a small grey wheel-and-axle piece in the lower right as the resize box; a stack of two or three loose bricks (blue, green) sits on the bottom-left corner. Widgets: three round 1x1 round-brick studs, red (close, x), green (zoom, +), blue (collapse, -), each with a single gloss pixel and a crisp 1 pixel white glyph. Push buttons are yellow 2x3 bricks with two studs on top, default ring is a red plate outline; checkboxes are 1x1 white plates with a red check, radios are round yellow studs; scroll thumb is a red 2x1 brick, scroll arrows are blue 1x1 plates with white arrowheads; progress bar is a row of green 1x1 bricks filling a grey channel. Palette: red #cc0000 / #ff3333 / #990000, yellow #ffcc00 / #ffff66 / #cc9900, blue #3366cc / #6699ff / #003399, green #339933 / #66cc66 / #006600, white #ffffff / #cccccc, grey #999999 / #666666, outline #330000, body #eeeeee. Style: a concept illustration of a classic Mac OS window theme mockup, 1990s Kaleidoscope style, crisp pixel-art look, flat plain background (solid mid-grey #8a94a8), no drop shadows, no photo textures. The illustration must show: one ACTIVE document window in front with a title bar reading the given title, three small title-bar widgets (close at left, zoom and collapse at right, each about 13 pixels, crisp and legible), a vertical and a horizontal scroll bar with arrows and a thumb, and inside the window two push buttons (Cancel, OK with a default ring), three checkboxes (one checked) and two radio buttons; plus one INACTIVE window partly behind it in a quieter palette. Window chrome must stay inside a rectangular footprint: ornaments may rise above the title bar as a taller top band, and may sit at the corners or repeat along edges, but nothing hangs outside the rectangle. Use a limited palette (under 40 colours), hard pixel edges, one light direction from the upper left, 1-pixel highlight rows and dark outlines rather than soft gradients. Reference images to pass in (for level of detail, chunkiness and how ornaments break the plain rectangle; do NOT copy their subject matter): /tmp/concepts/refs/animals.png, /tmp/concepts/refs/monkey-paradise.png, /tmp/concepts/refs/evolution.png, /tmp/concepts/refs/crayon-os.png. Aspect ratio 4:3.
