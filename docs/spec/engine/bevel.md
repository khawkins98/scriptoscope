## BV08 — diagnostics, errors and completion

C: Per-invocation collector preserves selection/admission/fallback order, repeated events, original requested identity and actual selected identity. Pattern selection remains an observation after successful pattern decode; completed pattern body enriches exactly its earlier selection, adding no second lookup. Mode5 completed step, completed pattern enrichment and legacyFacePaint publish only after border, labels and final clip succeed. Legacy record measures nonzero-alpha occupancy before glyphs under actual clip; it is not a write count or final surviving contribution. Band paints are recorded as each visible opaque-used band completes; earlier bands may remain on error, and their metadata is not evidence of a published buffer.

C: When the renderer fails, no completed body/legacy stage is included in the subsequently constructed error trace; callback and trace-construction failures have separate semantics below. Pattern selection may remain without bodyPaint; failed nonpattern bodyPaint steps are removed by wrapper. Error outcome has no width/height, null result is unavailable, successful result themed with actual retained buffer dimensions. Trace construction and recursive freeze occur outside the renderer try/catch. freezeRecord recursively enumerates Object.values and freezes recorded objects without cycle protection or defensive cloning; nested recorded provenance may still alias caller objects and may therefore be frozen. A getter, Object.values or freeze failure here rejects before onComplete, can partially freeze aliased objects, and replaces an already-caught renderer error. Preserve this existing behavior; do not add catches, cloning, cycle guards or a safe-freeze path. No fidelity claim is added.

C: withControlDiagnostics captures callback/correlation and renders once. If trace construction and recursive freezing succeed, it calls onComplete synchronously exactly once. Render success callback error rejects unchanged and does not invoke completion again; the callback has already received the completed themed trace, including its completed stages, which are not stripped retroactively. After successful trace construction/freezing on render failure, callback error is suppressed and the original renderer failure is rethrown. When trace construction/freezing fails, onComplete runs zero times and that failure wins instead. Errors before entering render/callback capture keep their present ordering (including requested-state getter exceptions). No trace when diagnostics absent, and no extra decode or metadata walk solely to manufacture diagnostics. Concurrent calls remain isolated except existing URL cache and caller-shared objects.
