The landing headline self-hosts the unmodified Latin WOFF2 subset of the regular
400 face supplied by Google Fonts (Instrument Serif v5), fetched 2026-10-18.
Source: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Instrument+Serif:ital@0;1&display=swap

Instrument Serif has naturally condensed glyphs. The headline uses native font
kerning with CSS letter-spacing and word-spacing, without horizontal scaling.
Cormorant Garamond remains the font for the italic invitation and wordmark.
