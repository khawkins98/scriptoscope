# Landing portrait

`portrait.jpg` is an original AI-generated source portrait made with the built-in
image-generation tool on 2026-10-18. It is not a photograph of a real identified
person and does not reproduce the person in the reference advertisement.
The generated PNG was converted to a 960px JPEG for delivery (about 269 KiB).

The user supplied a 1995 Apple New Media Forum ad as a layout reference.
`inspiration-apple-new-media-forum-1995.png` is that original user-supplied
advertisement, preserved for the visible inspiration credit on the page.
The website borrows its overlapping photographic panels, black field, large
serif headline, italic invitation, and centered print-ad rhythm. The five window
frames are rendered from the project's original scheme archives. Color and
crop treatments are CSS in `demo/index.html`; the photo contains no chrome.

## Generation prompt

Generate a photographic asset for a 1995-style multimedia advertisement. This is ONLY the source portrait photograph, NOT a poster or layout. Square composition, tightly cropped frontal head of a joyful androgynous young adult with short dark tousled hair, wide expressive eyes, mouth open in spontaneous joyful surprise/laughter with teeth visible. Face angled just slightly to viewer's right so left ear is visible. Head occupies nearly all of frame: hair at top, chin near bottom, ears on sides; plain deep black studio background. Black and white silver gelatin studio photography, high contrast, fine analog film grain, authentic mid-1990s editorial art direction, direct flash and sculptural shadows, slightly punk creative professional personality, human natural skin texture. Keep both eyes, visible ear, nose and open mouth distinctly in frame so individual rectangular crops can isolate each feature. No text, no typography, no logos, no frames, no collage, no graphic shapes. Save as a clean high quality portrait suitable for being segmented by CSS into separate colored panels on a website.
