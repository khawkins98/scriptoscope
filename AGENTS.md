# Repository instructions

Runtime implementers must first read
[`docs/spec/engine/IMPLEMENTER-BRIEF.md`](./docs/spec/engine/IMPLEMENTER-BRIEF.md),
then only their assigned safe family spec and the brief's exhaustive allowed paths.
Do not inspect original evidence, independent test/oracle sources, verifier reports
or historical research to fill gaps. Ask the spec author. General navigation and
maintenance tooling do not grant additional reading scope.

For other repository work, use [CLAUDE.md](./CLAUDE.md) for project norms and
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the task and role entry points.
Documentation changes follow the [editorial policy](./docs/doc-sweep.md#editorial-policy):
identify the reader and task, update its authoritative home, verify changed claims,
and preserve contracts, evidence and historical records according to their role.
